# Critical_Minerals_Supply_Risk_Analysis



Power BI analysis of supply concentration, export controls, price volatility
and demand pressure across 24 critical minerals. 

## Key findings
- 17 of 24 minerals depend on one country for at least 50% of production (2026).
- Controlled minerals rose from 3 (2023) to 9 (2025–26).
- Battery-mineral demand grows ~20–24% a year; graphite doubles in ~3.3 years.
- Eight minerals rate Critical on the vulnerability index; all are export-controlled.

## KPIs
Supply Concentration (HHI) · Export-Control Exposure · Price Volatility ·
Demand Pressure vs Concentration · Strategic Vulnerability Index

## Tools
Power BI (Power Query, DAX) 

## Data
1,548 rows, 24 minerals, 35 countries, 2015–2026. 


The refined-share column appears formula-generated, so mining-vs-refining
results are indicative. HHI is approximate (top producers only). 2026 is a
partial year.
